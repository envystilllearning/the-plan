The entry plan turns listing photos into 30-second video tours for realtors: free samples to 300 agents, $15 per video, then batch production and a $99 template sale. It won a three-model judge panel (aggregate 6.70 of 12, ahead of a booking-page plan at 6.30 and a data-cleaning plan at 5.00).
